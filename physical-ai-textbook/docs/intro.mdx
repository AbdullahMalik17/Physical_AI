# Physical AI: From Simulation to Reality

Welcome to the **Physical AI Textbook Platform**! This interactive learning resource will guide you through the exciting journey of building humanoid robots that can successfully transition from simulation to the real world.

## What is "Sim-to-Real"?

The **Sim-to-Real** concept is at the heart of modern robotics development. It's the process of:

1. **Designing** your robot in a simulation environment
2. **Training** AI models in safe, controlled virtual spaces
3. **Testing** behaviors without risk of physical damage
4. **Transferring** learned skills to real-world robots

This approach dramatically reduces development costs, accelerates iteration cycles, and ensures safer deployment of robotic systems.

## What You'll Learn

Throughout this textbook, you'll master three core pillars:

### 🤖 **ROS 2 (Robot Operating System 2)**
Learn the industry-standard framework for building modular, scalable robot applications. ROS 2 provides the communication infrastructure that powers everything from simple sensors to complex humanoid behaviors.

### 🎮 **NVIDIA Isaac Sim**
Explore photorealistic simulation environments where you can test your robots in scenarios that mirror real-world physics. Isaac Sim allows you to iterate quickly and safely before deploying to hardware.

### 💬 **AI-Powered Learning**
Leverage advanced AI techniques including Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) to enhance your understanding and get instant answers to your robotics questions.
