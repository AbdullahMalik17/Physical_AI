## Vision for Physical AI

I believe that the future of robotics lies in the seamless integration of high-fidelity simulation and real-world hardware. My mission with this book is to democratize the knowledge required to build, train, and deploy humanoid robots that are as capable as they are intelligent.

## Professional Background

*   **Robotics Engineering:** Focused on humanoid locomotion and control systems.
*   **AI Research:** Specializing in Sim-to-Real transfer and Embodied Intelligence.
*   **Open Source Advocate:** Committed to building transparent and accessible technology.
